# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students at North East universities (Durham, Newcastle, Northumbria, Sunderland, Teesside), plus some employed or unemployed users in the same region. They check in once a day, usually on a phone, often tired, sometimes stressed about exams. A check-in takes seconds: a short voice note or a few typed lines. Once a week they come back to hear their week as a short piece of music and read what their own data shows.

## Product Purpose

Cadence (working name Smart Diary) turns a term of quick diary check-ins into structured wellbeing data (mood, sleep, stress, study), explains real patterns in plain English, gives exam-aware advice, and composes each week into a piece of music. It is wellbeing support, not therapy. Success: a student keeps checking in through exam season because it is quick, feels like theirs, and the weekly composition is something they want to hear.

## Brand Personality

Musical, playful, alive. The interface should feel like an instrument rather than a form: rhythm, sound and colour carry meaning (a calm week looks and sounds calm, an exam week looks and sounds tense). Copy is plain, warm and a little regional, never clinical and never hype. Playful does not mean silly: on a hard day the UI stays gentle.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI or SaaS app: grey sidebar shell, emoji mood pills, stock card stacks, sparkle "Smart Assistant ✨" buttons, placeholder dashboard stats.
- Anything that implies the AI made a medical judgement: scores presented as diagnoses.
- Guilt mechanics: streak shaming, nagging about missed days.

## Design Principles

1. **The week is a composition.** Visual structure echoes musical structure (beats, bars, tempo), so the theme is felt in the UI, not just bolted on as an audio player.
2. **Seconds, not minutes.** The check-in is the primary action on every visit and must be reachable and finishable with one thumb.
3. **Show their data, not decoration.** Every colour, shape or number on screen comes from what the user actually logged.
4. **Gentle on hard days.** Playfulness steps back when the data is heavy; crisis help is always plain and easy to find.
5. **Honest about the machine.** Label AI output and offline mode clearly; never pretend rules are AI.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard access. Phone-first with large tap targets. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Never rely on colour alone to carry mood or stress (pair with labels or shape). Voice check-in must have a typed alternative.
