How to read it

  • Colour is the mood you chose. It sets the key: brighter for happy, minor for stressed.
  • Height is hours of sleep. Less sleep plays faster.
  • Stripes mean stress of 7 or more. It adds tension to the week’s sound.
  • Dots are study or work hours. More hours, busier rhythm.
  • Dashed means not recorded. Missing values stay missing; nothing is guessed.
