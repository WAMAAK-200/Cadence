How it works

Seconds a day, not another chore.

  1. 1

    Say how you are

    Pick a mood, or skip it. One thumb, no forms.

    “How are you, really?”
  2. 2

    Get a note back

    A short, gentle reply that fits the mood you picked, with a breathing pause if you want one.

    “Want to take a minute first?”
  3. 3

    Write, or talk, if you like

    Type a few lines or use your voice. Cadence suggests the details from your words, and you check them before anything is saved.

    “Help fill this in”

The weekly composition

Your data, played back to you.

Once a week, the days you wrote become a short piece of music. A calm week sounds calm. An exam week sounds like an exam week. Press a button to hear each idea.

Mood Key

Brighter moods play in a major key; harder weeks move into minor and modal colours.

Sleep Tempo

Good nights give an unhurried tempo. Short nights speed everything up.

Stress Tension

Low stress stays smooth. High stress adds an edge you can hear.

Study & work Rhythm

Light days are sparse. Long library days get busy.

Patterns, in plain English

What your own week is telling you.

After nights under six hours, your stress the next day tends to be higher. It might be worth protecting sleep in the week before your exams.

Example insight

The pattern is worked out in code from what you logged. The wording explains it. If there isn’t enough data yet, Cadence says so rather than guessing.

Students can add exam dates, so advice shifts from building habits early in term, to protecting sleep before exams, to recovering after.

Privacy & care

Honest about what it is, gentle on hard days.

  • Wellbeing support, not therapy

    Cadence never diagnoses or scores you. It shows what you logged and points to real help when it matters.

  • Your words stay yours

    Entries are encrypted. Without an account they stay on your device, and you can delete them whenever you like.

  • Clear about the machine

    AI suggestions are labelled. Without a connection to the AI service, a simpler offline helper takes over, and it says so.

  • No guilt mechanics

    Missed a week? Nothing breaks and nothing nags. Days you didn’t write are left empty, never filled in.

Having a really hard time? Samaritans are free, any time, on 116 123. In immediate danger, call 999.
